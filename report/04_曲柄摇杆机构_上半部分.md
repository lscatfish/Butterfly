$$l_1\cos\theta_1+l_2\cos\theta_2=d_{AB}+l_3\cos\theta_3 \tag{4-4}$$

$$l_1\sin\theta_1+l_2\sin\theta_2=l_3\sin\theta_3 \tag{4-5}$$
